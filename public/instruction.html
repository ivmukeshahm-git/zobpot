About This Player

This video player is specifically designed for portrait (vertical) videos with a 9:16 aspect ratio. It features custom controls optimized for mobile devices and vertical viewing.

How to Use This Player

  1. Replace YOUR_PORTRAIT_VIDEO_FILE_PATH_HERE with the path to your portrait video file.
  2. If your video is in the same folder, just use the filename (e.g., src="my-portrait-video.mp4").
  3. For best results, use MP4 format with H.264 video codec.
  4. The player is responsive and will work on both mobile and desktop devices.
